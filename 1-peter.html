1 Peter

1 Peter is part of the Bible story and points to God’s redemptive plan fulfilled in Christ.

Key verse: Psalm 119:105

Simple Explanation

This lesson gives you a Bible-school foundation in simple English. Read slowly, check the Scripture, and ask the Holy Spirit to illuminate the Word without contradicting the Word.

Key Ideas

  • What this topic teaches about God.
  • What this topic teaches about humanity and sin.
  • How this topic points to Jesus Christ.
  • How the believer should respond in faith, obedience, prayer, and love.

Pentecostal Application

We believe the Bible is the final authority, and the Holy Spirit helps believers understand, obey, preach, and live Scripture with power and holiness.

Homework

  • Read the key verse in context.
  • Write a 5-sentence summary in your own words.
  • Pray: “Lord, teach me Your Word and help me obey.”
