Bible Timeline

A simple overview from creation to restoration.

Creation

Genesis 1–2

God creates the heavens, earth, humanity, and everything good.

Fall

Genesis 3

Sin enters the world, but God promises a coming Savior.

Patriarchs

Genesis 12–50

Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph carry the promise.

Exodus

Exodus–Deuteronomy

God delivers Israel and gives the covenant law.

Kingdom

Samuel–Kings

Saul, David, Solomon, division, decline, and exile.

Jesus

Gospels

The Son of God comes, dies, and rises again.

Church

Acts–Epistles

The Spirit empowers the church to preach the gospel.

Restoration

Revelation

Jesus returns, judges evil, and makes all things new.
